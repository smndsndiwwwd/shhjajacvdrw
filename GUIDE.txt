🏏 VIRTUAL CM (VIRTUAL CAREER MODE) - COMPLETE PLAYERS GUIDE 🏏
================================================================================

Welcome, player! This guide explains all the features, math, standing systems, and 
match engine matchups in Virtual Career Mode so you can rise from an Academy raw 
recruit to an International Cricket Legend!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 👤 PLAYER VITALS & SYSTEM STATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your profile (/profile) tracks six main vitals that change dynamically as you 
play, train, and make career decisions:

⚡ ENERGY:
   - Your physical battery. Maxes out at 100.
   - Both training drills (/train) and playing matches (/play) consume 15 Energy.
   - Energy refills passively over time. Keep an eye on your battery!
   
🔥 CONFIDENCE:
   - Ranges from 10 to 100.
   - Changes ball-by-ball during a match.
   - Hitting boundaries (+4s/6s) boosts confidence (+6).
   - Playing dot balls reduces confidence. Getting dismissed reduces it by -15.
   - High confidence increases your matchup rating, making boundaries easier. 
     Low confidence increases the risk of edges or getting bowled.
     
📈 Recent Form:
   - Ranges from 20 to 100.
   - Matches your recent performances. Winning a match raises form by +10. 
     Losing a match reduces form by -10.
   - Higher form increases contract bids from clubs!
   
🎖️ REPUTATION:
   - Your fame and standing in the cricket world. Starts at 0.
   - Critical for unlocking major club contract offers and brand sponsorships.
   - How to earn Reputation:
     1. Play Chapter Matches: Completing chapters yields large reputation boosts.
     2. Match Victories: Defeating opponents increases reputation.
     3. Training Regimes: Consistently practicing grants minor reputation.
     4. Story Choices: Post-match random events have choices that can raise or 
        lower your reputation (e.g. arguing with coach vs apologizing).
        
🌟 SKILL POINTS (SP):
   - Earned by leveling up. Each Level Up gives you +1 Skill Point.
   - Spend SP in the Skill Tree (/skills view & /skills unlock) to get passive 
     perks (like power timing windows, yorker defence, spinner sweeps).
     
🪙 COINS:
   - The bot's primary currency. Use coins to buy equipment from the shop (/equipment shop)
     or request academy transfers.
   - Earn coins by playing matches, completing chapters, signing contract salaries,
     and securing brand sponsorships.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. 🎓 TRAINING ACADEMIES & TRANSFERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every player begins at a Cricket Academy. Academies provide passive training multipliers:
  - Elite Cricket Academy: +8% Batting XP, +5% Fitness XP (Arthur)
  - National High Performance Centre: +5% Batting/Bowling, +3% Stamina (Shastri)
  - Dustbowl Spin Academy: +10% Spin Batting & Spin Bowling XP (Murali)
  - Lightning Pace Foundry: +8% Pace Bowling, +5% Power XP (Donald)

Transferring Academies:
  - If you want a different specialty, run /academy.
  - You can request a transfer to any other academy once per season for a transfer fee 
    of 200 Coins.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. 🏋️ TRAINING REGIMES (/train)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Train to boost your attributes and gain XP! Each session costs 15 Energy & 1 Token:
  - Nets Batting Practice: Boosts Batting Attributes (Power, Timing, Technique, Defense).
  - Target Bowling Drills: Boosts Bowling Attributes (Swing, Spin Bowling, Accuracy, Variations).
  - Gym Workout: Boosts Physical Attributes (Fitness, Stamina, Power).
  - Sprint & Fielding Drills: Boosts Athletics (Running Speed, Fielding, Reflexes, Throw Accuracy).

Each session awards +100 Base XP (modified by academy multipliers) and attributes boost.
Leveling up gives you +1 Skill Point and adds a permanent +1 boost to ALL attributes!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. 💼 CLUB CONTRACTS & DRAFTS (/contracts)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Levels 1-3: You play under an academy stipend (20 Coins / Match).
  - Level 4+: Request club bids using /contracts.
  - Three local, state, or franchise clubs will bid for your signature.
  - Your level and reputation determine the salary bid.
  - Signing a contract awards you a set salary in coins for every match you play!
  - Contracts last for a set number of seasons. When your contract expires, you return 
    to academy status until you request new bids.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. 👟 BRAND SPONSORSHIPS (/sponsors)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sponsors are premium endorsement contracts you sign to get massive rewards:
  - Check eligible sponsors in the /sponsors menu.
  - Unlocking sponsors requires meeting Reputation milestones.
  - Signing a sponsor gives you:
    1. A large, immediate Sign-on Coin Bonus.
    2. Premium Sponsor Gear delivered to your inventory (grips, bats, shoes).
  - Signing a new sponsor replaces your current sponsor. Build your reputation 
    to attract high-tier brands (like Apex or WoodWorks Willow)!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. 🎒 EQUIPMENT SHOP & INVENTORY (/inventory & /equipment)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Visit the shop (/equipment shop) to buy gear using your coins.
  - Items are categorized into Bats, Shoes, Grips, Gloves, and Pads.
  - Rarity Tiers: Common (white), Rare (blue), Epic (purple), Legendary (yellow).
  - Equipping Gear: View your equipment bag (/inventory) and select an item to equip.
    Only one item of each type can be equipped at a time.
  - Equipped gear adds passive attribute boosts (+Power, +Running) to your matchup rolls.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. ⚔️ PVP CHALLENGE MODE DUELS (/challenge)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Want to prove you are the best batsman or bowler? Duel other players in real-time!
  - duel: Challenge any player in the guild to a 1-over (6 balls) shootout.
    - Energy Cost: 15 Energy from both players.
    - Cooldown: 30 minutes.
    - Shootout: Both players play a simulated 1-over shootout. Highest score wins!
    - Rewards:
      - Winner: +200 Coins, +150 XP, +10 Reputation, +15 Recent Form.
      - Loser: +50 Coins, +50 XP, -5 Reputation, -5 Recent Form.
      - Tie: +100 Coins, +100 XP to both players.
  - stats: Check your separate Challenge Mode stats (played, won, lost, runs, boundaries).
  - leaderboard: View the global duels leaderboard rendered with beautiful image graphics!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. 🏏 CRICKET MATCH ENGINE & STRATEGY (/play)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Matches are simulated ball-by-ball. Batting and bowling success is based on a 
hidden matchup rating between the ball delivery type and the shot type selected.

⚾ BATTING MATCHUPS:
   - Yorker delivery: Best countered by DEFENSIVE SHOT or STRAIGHT DRIVE.
     DO NOT Slog, Pull, or Hook against Yorkers (high bowled/LBW risk).
   - Bouncer / Short ball: Countered by PULL SHOT, HOOK SHOT, or UPPER CUT.
     DO NOT Drive or Sweep against bouncers (high catch risk).
   - Spin (Offbreak/Legbreak/Googly): Countered by SWEEP SHOT or REVERSE SWEEP.
     DO NOT Cut spin balls (high edge/stumped risk).
   - Seam / Swing delivery: Countered by STRAIGHT DRIVE or COVER DRIVE.
     DO NOT Slog or Cut swing deliveries (high slips catch risk).

🔴 BOWLING MATCHUPS:
   - Select Outswing, Yorker, Bouncer, or Googly to trick the batsman.
   - Aligning your delivery to the pitch type (Standard, Grass, Dustbowl) increases 
     the batsman's mistake rate.
   - High Accuracy and Variations attributes result in dot balls and wickets!

🏆 CHAPTER PROGRESSION:
   - Run /chapters to view the campaign list.
   - Play Chapter matches to progress through the 20 levels. Each level increases in 
     difficulty and target scores, culminating in representing your country in the 
     International Tier chapters.
